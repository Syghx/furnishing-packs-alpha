        Before you order.

        Can I make changes to a pack?

        Yes. The packs cover what most properties need, but we can add or remove pieces. Put the changes in your quote request and we'll adjust the pack.

        Do you deliver nationwide?

        Yes. We furnish properties all over the UK, completely hands off for the owner.

        How much is delivery?

        Nothing. Delivery, assembly and packaging removal are included in every pack price.

        How quickly can you deliver?

        In as little as 48 hours. You choose the week that suits the property, and we confirm the day and time with you.

        Do you build the furniture on delivery?

        Yes. Our team assembles every piece in the room it belongs in and takes all the packaging away.

        Are the prices on this page final?

        They're our published prices, per property and excluding VAT. Your quote confirms the final figure, including any changes you ask for.
