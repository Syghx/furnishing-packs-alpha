Two directions I considered, and why the doorway wins

Outline doorway. Lighter and more delicate, but the thin line disappears at tab size.
Monogram in an arch. Clear, but letters in a shape are what everyone does; it says less.
Recommended
Doorway and pack. One idea, our own shape, bold enough for a van, a jacket or a 16-pixel tab.

Using it

  • Colours: ink #111213 and champagne #C9A87C; reversed in white on dark. The square is always champagne.
  • Name: lower case in Outfit ExtraBold, as now, ending in the champagne square instead of a full stop.
  • Space: keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of the square.
  • Before print: have a designer convert the name to outlines and add CMYK and Pantone matches for the van and printed items.
